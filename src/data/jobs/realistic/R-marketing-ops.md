# Marketing Operations Manager

Company: Northgate Health Technologies
Location: Remote (US)
Seniority: Mid-Senior level

Northgate Health Technologies builds scheduling and patient-engagement software used by
over 400 clinics nationwide. We're a Series C company of about 600 people, growing quickly,
and we care a lot about doing right by our customers and each other. Benefits include fully
paid medical for employees, unlimited PTO, a home office stipend, 12 weeks parental leave,
and an annual learning budget.

Role overview
We're hiring a Marketing Operations Manager to own the systems and reporting that our
demand generation team runs on. You'll be the technical backbone of the marketing org.

Responsibilities
- Own and administer our marketing automation platform (HubSpot) end to end
- Manage the integration and data flow between HubSpot and Salesforce
- Define and maintain lead lifecycle stages, scoring models and routing rules
- Build and maintain dashboards reporting on funnel performance, channel ROI and pipeline
  contribution
- Own UTM governance and multi-touch attribution reporting
- Partner with demand gen on campaign QA, launch and post-campaign analysis
- Run and interpret A/B tests across email and landing pages
- Manage data hygiene, deduplication and enrichment processes
- Support quarterly planning with historical performance analysis

Required qualifications
- 4+ years in marketing operations, demand generation or a closely related function
- Hands-on administration of a marketing automation platform (HubSpot, Marketo or Pardot)
- Working knowledge of Salesforce, including objects, fields and reports
- Demonstrated experience with lead lifecycle and routing design
- Strong analytical skills; comfort working with large data sets
- Experience building executive-facing dashboards

Preferred qualifications
- SQL proficiency for querying a data warehouse
- Experience with Looker, Tableau or similar BI tooling
- Familiarity with attribution modeling approaches
- Healthcare or regulated-industry experience
- Experience supporting an ABM motion

Northgate is committed to building a diverse team and strongly encourages applications
from candidates of all backgrounds.
