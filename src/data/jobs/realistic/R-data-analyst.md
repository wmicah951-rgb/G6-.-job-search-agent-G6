# Data Analyst, Commercial Analytics

Company: Verdant Grocery Partners
Location: Remote — US (occasional travel to Denver HQ, roughly once a quarter)

Who we are
Verdant Grocery Partners operates a network of regional grocery brands serving communities
across the Mountain West. With 14,000 associates and 190 stores, we blend the scale of a
national operator with the feel of a neighborhood store. Our Commercial Analytics team sits
inside Merchandising and is responsible for the reporting that category managers use daily.

What you'll be doing
- Build, maintain and improve dashboards used by category and pricing managers
- Write SQL to pull and shape data from our enterprise data warehouse
- Partner with merchandising stakeholders to translate vague questions into analyses
- Support the weekly business review with accurate, on-time reporting
- Investigate data quality issues and work with engineering to resolve them
- Automate recurring manual reporting wherever possible
- Present findings to non-technical audiences in a clear, decision-ready format

What you'll need
- 2+ years of experience in an analytics or reporting role
- Strong SQL — you should be comfortable with joins, aggregations and window functions
- Experience building dashboards in a BI tool (we use Power BI)
- Advanced Excel
- Bachelor's degree in a quantitative discipline, or equivalent practical experience
- Excellent communication skills and comfort presenting to stakeholders

Bonus points for
- Python or R for analysis and automation
- Retail, grocery or CPG experience
- Exposure to experimentation or A/B testing
- Familiarity with dbt or a similar transformation tool
- Snowflake experience

Compensation: $78,000 - $95,000 depending on experience, plus annual bonus target of 8%.
