# Financial Analyst

Company: Kestrel Manufacturing Group
Location: Charlotte, NC (Hybrid — 3 days onsite)
Employment type: Full-time

About Kestrel
Kestrel Manufacturing Group is a leading supplier of precision components to the aerospace
and automotive sectors. Founded in 1974, we operate 11 facilities across North America and
employ over 2,800 people. We are proud of our culture of ownership, safety and continuous
improvement, and we offer comprehensive medical, dental and vision coverage, a 401(k) with
company match, tuition reimbursement, paid parental leave and an employee stock purchase plan.

The Opportunity
Reporting to the FP&A Manager, the Financial Analyst will support budgeting, forecasting
and management reporting for our Southeast operations. This is a high-visibility role that
partners directly with plant leadership.

What you'll do
- Support the monthly close process, including preparation of journal entries and account
  reconciliations
- Prepare monthly budget-versus-actual reporting packages and investigate material variances
- Maintain and improve the rolling forecast model
- Partner with plant controllers to understand cost drivers and margin performance
- Build ad-hoc analyses for senior leadership, including scenario and sensitivity modeling
- Assist with the annual operating plan and quarterly reforecast cycles
- Identify opportunities to automate recurring reporting
- Support internal and external audit requests as needed

What we're looking for
- Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics or a related field
- 1-3 years of relevant experience (internships and co-ops count)
- Advanced Excel skills, including lookups, PivotTables and modeling
- Experience with variance analysis and month-end close
- Strong written and verbal communication skills
- High attention to detail and comfort working to deadlines
- Ability to work cross-functionally with non-finance stakeholders

Nice to have
- Experience with an ERP system such as NetSuite, SAP or Oracle
- Exposure to Power BI or Tableau
- Progress toward CPA or CFA
- SQL familiarity
